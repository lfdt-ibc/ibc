## Standards Committee

The Standards Committee is the IBC Technical Steering Committee (TSC). Its members are listed in [TSC_MEMBERS.md](../TSC_MEMBERS.md), and are added and removed as [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) describes.

The TSC members are the Editors of the specifications, and only they approve changes to the specifications, as set out in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#specifications).
